簡介Biography

鄒承廷老師現任國立中央大學企業管理學系助理教授，合聘於永續與綠能科技研究學院，並擔任國際企業管理碩士學位學程（IMBA）副主任。

進入學界前，鄒老師擁有逾十年產業經驗，職涯橫跨傳統製造業與新興科技產業。早年任職於 Boeing 與日月光集團，其後投入汽車產業，先後在 Ford 及 Fiat Chrysler Automobiles（現 Stellantis）負責產品規劃與管理工作；電動車產業快速崛起之際加入小鵬汽車，之後轉往小鵬匯天（ARIDGE）參與飛行汽車等新興交通科技的開發。其產業歷練以產品策略、技術規劃與國際市場發展為主軸。

在學術研究方面，鄒老師長期關注技術變革與產業發展的關係，探討企業如何因應新興科技所帶來的競爭環境變化，以及政府政策與制度環境在產業轉型中所扮演的角色。近年研究涵蓋汽車電動化、先進空中交通、企業國際化與能源轉型，成果發表於 Technological Forecasting and Social Change、Science and Public Policy、Tourism Geographies 及 Asian Journal of Technology Innovation 等國際期刊，目前並主持國家科學及技術委員會研究計畫。

鄒老師畢業於英國格拉斯哥大學亞當斯密商學院，取得策略與科技管理博士學位。其教育背景橫跨工程、管理與資料分析，先後於國立成功大學、國立政治大學、法國 ESCP 商學院、美國華盛頓大學及喬治亞理工學院取得學位，兼具跨領域與跨國的求學及工作經歷。

Dr Cheng-Ting Tsou is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Business Administration at National Central University, with a joint appointment in the Graduate College of Sustainability and Green Energy. He also serves as Deputy Programme Director of the International MBA (IMBA).

Before entering academia, Dr Tsou spent more than ten years in industry, spanning traditional manufacturing and emerging technology sectors. He began his career at Boeing and ASE Group before moving into the automotive industry, where he held product planning and management roles at Ford and Fiat Chrysler Automobiles (now Stellantis). As the electric vehicle industry accelerated, he joined XPeng Motors and later ARIDGE, contributing to the development of flying cars and other emerging mobility technologies. His industry work centred on product strategy, technology planning and international market development.

His research examines the relationship between technological change and industrial development, focusing on how firms respond to shifts in competition brought by emerging technologies and on the role of government policy and institutional environments in industrial transitions. His recent work covers automotive electrification, advanced air mobility, internationalisation and energy transitions, and has been published in international journals including Technological Forecasting and Social Change, Science and Public Policy, Tourism Geographies and the Asian Journal of Technology Innovation. He currently leads research projects funded by Taiwan's National Science and Technology Council.

Dr Tsou holds a PhD in Strategy and Technology Management from the Adam Smith Business School, University of Glasgow. His education spans engineering, management and data analytics, with degrees from National Cheng Kung University, National Chengchi University, ESCP Business School, the University of Washington and the Georgia Institute of Technology, giving him a cross-disciplinary and international background in both study and work.
